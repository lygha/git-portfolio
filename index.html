About Me

I’m a Computer Engineering undergraduate who genuinely enjoys building things and figuring out how systems work — from code to hardware. I’m still learning, and I don’t pretend to know everything, but I’m curious, motivated, and serious about improving my skills.

Right now, I’m focusing on React and backend development, trying to understand how real-world applications are designed, built, and scaled. I enjoy working on projects that combine logic, structure, and problem-solving, whether that’s a web app, a system-level project, or something that connects software with hardware.

I’m also interested in AI, IoT, and cybersecurity, and I see my learning path as something broad rather than limited to a single field. My goal isn’t to rush into a title like “full-stack developer,” but to build a strong foundation, gain hands-on experience, and grow into it naturally over time.

I learn best by doing — experimenting, breaking things, fixing them, and improving. I’m always looking to expand my knowledge, take on new challenges, and work on projects that actually solve problems and just work.
